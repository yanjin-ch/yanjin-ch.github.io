cps-backup — Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Last updated / 最後更新：2026-11-12

cps-backup is a private backup tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google account. There are no other users.
cps-backup 是擁有者自用的私人備份工具，只用於擁有者自己的 Google 帳號，沒有其他使用者。

Google user data accessed / 存取的 Google 使用者資料

Only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope: the files that cps-backup itself created in the owner's Google Drive. It does not access any other Drive file, email, contacts, profile or other Google data.
只使用 drive.file 權限：僅限 cps-backup 自己在擁有者雲端硬碟建立的檔案。不存取其他雲端硬碟檔案、郵件、聯絡人、個人資料或其他 Google 資料。

How it is used / 使用方式

To upload the owner's encrypted backup files, download them back to verify they are intact, list them, and delete the tool's own expired backups. Nothing else.
用於上傳擁有者的加密備份、下載回來核對、列出檔案，以及刪除工具自己過期的備份。沒有其他用途。

Storage and sharing / 保存與分享

Backup files stay in the owner's own Google Drive and are encrypted before upload. The Google authorization is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's own accounts. No Google user data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, used for advertising, or used to train AI/ML models.
備份檔只存在擁有者自己的雲端硬碟，上傳前已加密。Google 授權只以加密秘密形式保存在擁有者自己的帳戶中。不出售、不分享、不轉移給任何第三方，不用於廣告，也不用於訓練 AI／機器學習模型。

cps-backup's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access / 撤銷授權

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already in Drive remain under the owner's control.
擁有者可隨時在上述網址撤銷授權；已存在雲端硬碟的備份仍由擁有者自行管理。
